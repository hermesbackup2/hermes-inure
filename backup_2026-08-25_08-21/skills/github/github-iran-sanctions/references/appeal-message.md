# GitHub Iran Suspension — Appeal Message Template

This is the exact message text that successfully moved a suspended Iran-based
account into the Support review queue (verified working: the user submitted it
via the support.github.com modal and received the "Proceed with submitting this
support ticket" confirmation).

GitHub's own suspension modal confirms the key policy: Iran is covered by
GitHub's OFAC license for BOTH public and private cloud services, so the
"convert repos to public" path is NOT required for Iran. Focus the appeal on a
manual flag review, not on repo visibility.

## Subject
Appeal: Account <USERNAME> suspended in error — request flag review

## Body
Hello GitHub Support,

My account **<USERNAME>** is currently suspended and I cannot sign in. Based on
GitHub's published trade-control policy, Iran-based users are eligible for both
public and private services under GitHub's OFAC license, so my suspension was
likely an **erroneous flag** rather than a sanctions restriction.

I am an individual developer based in Iran. I am:
- NOT a Specially Designated National (SDN)
- NOT a government official
- NOT a blocked/denied party

My account was likely flagged due to **IP-address signals** (I use VPN/proxy
connections due to regional network restrictions) or **payment-history
patterns**, not due to any actual sanctions violation.

I respectfully request a manual review of the account flag. Please let me know
what **verification information** you need from me to confirm I am not in a
restricted category, and I will provide it promptly.

I am happy to cooperate with any identity or verification checks required to
resolve this. Thank you for your time.

Best regards,
<USERNAME>

## Notes
- Replace <USERNAME> with the actual GitHub handle.
- Submit at support.github.com or reply to the suspension email.
- After submitting, watch Spam for the verification email — response is usually
  a few business days.
- Do NOT create a new account on a VPN to bypass — risks faster ban (ToS violation).
- Do NOT change repo visibility before the flag is reviewed.
